THE TOP 10 FASHION BRANDS

Explore ten major global fashion brands ranked by reported brand value in the 2026 FashionUnited index. Each brand has its own identity, history, and influence on the fashion industry.

01 / NUMBER ONE

Hermès

France

Founded in 1837, Hermès is renowned for exceptional craftsmanship, leather goods, silk scarves, and timeless luxury accessories.

Luxury Fashion & Accessories
02 / NUMBER TWO

Louis Vuitton

France

Established in 1854, Louis Vuitton is famous for its travel heritage, signature monogram, luxury handbags, clothing, and accessories.

Luxury Fashion
03 / NUMBER THREE

Chanel

France

Founded by Gabrielle Chanel, the house is celebrated for the Chanel suit, the Little Black Dress, haute couture, and timeless elegance.

Luxury Fashion & Beauty
04 / NUMBER FOUR

Nike

United States

Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports, Nike became a global sportswear leader, known for athletic footwear, apparel, innovation, and iconic sports collaborations.

Sportswear & Footwear
05 / NUMBER FIVE

Zara

Spain

Founded in 1975, Zara is known for bringing contemporary fashion trends to a global audience through its fast-fashion retail model.

Fashion Retail
06 / NUMBER SIX

Adidas

Germany

Founded in 1949, Adidas is recognized worldwide for its three-stripe identity, sportswear, sneakers, and collaborations that connect sport and street fashion.

Sportswear & Footwear
07 / NUMBER SEVEN

Cartier

France

Founded in 1847, Cartier is celebrated for fine jewelry, watches, iconic designs, and a long tradition of luxury craftsmanship.

Luxury Jewelry & Watches
08 / NUMBER EIGHT

UNIQLO

Japan

Known for its LifeWear philosophy, UNIQLO focuses on simple, functional, comfortable clothing and innovative everyday essentials.

Everyday Fashion & Retail
09 / NUMBER NINE

Rolex

Switzerland

Founded in 1905, Rolex is one of the world's most recognized luxury watchmakers, known for precision, craftsmanship, and iconic timepieces.

Luxury Watches
10 / NUMBER TEN

Dior

France

Founded in 1946 by Christian Dior, the fashion house became famous for its New Look silhouette, haute couture, ready-to-wear, and luxury accessories.

Luxury Fashion & Beauty
